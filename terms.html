Purpose and access

YouTube Analytics Sync is a personal desktop utility for its owner to retrieve and analyze reports for a YouTube channel they own and authorize. It is not a public analytics service and does not offer account registration, subscription plans, or paid services.

Owner responsibilities

The owner controls the computer, Google authorization, scheduled runs, retained reports, and backups. Use is limited to authorized channel data and must comply with applicable Google and YouTube terms and policies. The utility does not grant access to channels the owner is not authorized to access.

Report limitations

Reports depend on Google API availability, permissions, processing delays, supported metrics, and quotas. Revenue values are estimates and may be revised. Missing data is not proof of zero activity or zero revenue. API reports do not establish YouTube Partner Program eligibility or replace YouTube Studio's official monetization status.

Availability and control

The utility is provided as a personal tool without service-level commitments. Scheduled synchronization requires an available computer, network connectivity, and valid Google authorization. The owner can stop using it, disable scheduled runs, revoke access, and delete local data at any time.

Privacy and independence

Data handling is described in the privacy policy. This project is independent and is not affiliated with, sponsored by, or endorsed by Google or YouTube. Google and YouTube remain responsible for their own services and policies.
