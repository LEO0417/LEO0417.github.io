YouTube Analytics Sync downloads a channel owner's YouTube reports for their private analysis. It is operated by its owner for personal use.

What the utility does

With the channel owner's permission, the utility uses the official YouTube Data API and YouTube Analytics API to retrieve channel information, uploaded-video metadata, views, watch time, subscription changes, traffic sources, geography, and available estimated revenue reports.

It saves reports as local CSV and JSON files, maintains validated snapshots and backups, and can refresh those reports on a schedule while the owner's computer is running.

Read-only access

The utility requests permission to view the owner's channel, analytics, and monetary analytics. It does not upload or edit videos, change thumbnails, post comments, or modify channel settings.

A personal tool, not a hosted data service

This website provides application information and policies. It has no sign-in form and does not collect channel credentials or analytics. Google authorization takes place in the owner's desktop workflow; reports and authorization tokens are stored on the owner's computer.

Read the privacy policy to understand data access, local storage, retention, and revocation. The terms of use describe the personal-use scope.

Independent personal project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or YouTube.
