Privacy policy

Effective November 7, 2026

YouTube Analytics Sync is a desktop utility operated by its owner solely for personal channel analysis. This policy covers the utility and this informational website.

Data accessed and purpose

After the channel owner grants Google OAuth permission, the utility reads channel identifiers and profile information, channel statistics, uploaded-video identifiers, titles and publication dates, and YouTube Analytics reports. Reports can include views, engaged views, watch time, subscriptions gained and lost, traffic sources, country-level audience data, and available estimated revenue and advertising metrics.

Access is used only to create, refresh, back up, and analyze the owner's channel reports. Revenue access requires the corresponding read-only permission and availability through Google's API.

Permissions

The utility requests these Google permissions:

  • youtube.readonly — view channel information and uploaded-video metadata.
  • yt-analytics.readonly — view channel analytics.
  • yt-analytics-monetary.readonly — view estimated revenue and advertising reports.

It does not request permission to publish, edit, or delete YouTube content.

Storage and security

OAuth client information, access and refresh tokens, reports, and backups are stored on the owner's computer under their operating-system account. Credential directories and files use restricted owner-only permissions. Credentials are not committed to source repositories or published on this website.

The utility connects to Google's official HTTPS API and authorization endpoints. This website does not receive the utility's authorization callbacks, tokens, reports, or channel data.

Sharing and limited use

The utility does not sell Google user data, use it for advertising, or use it to train AI models. Reports stay local unless the owner explicitly provides selected reports to another application or service for analysis. The owner controls such transfers; those services have their own applicable policies.

The utility's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention, revocation, and deletion

Local reports, snapshots, credential backups, and refresh tokens are retained until the owner removes them. New synchronization runs preserve earlier snapshots; the utility does not automatically delete historical backups.

The owner can stop scheduled runs and revoke the utility's access at any time in Google Account connections. Revocation stops future authorized access but does not erase previously saved local files. To remove stored data, the owner must also delete the utility's local reports, credentials, and their backups.

Website hosting

These static information pages use GitHub Pages. They contain no advertising, analytics scripts, account forms, or application cookies. GitHub may process visitor technical information, including IP addresses, to operate and secure its hosting service under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Support and changes

There is no public account registration or external-user support service. The personal operator can review configuration, revoke permissions, or remove local data directly. Questions about this informational policy can be raised through the website's issue tracker; do not post credentials or private channel data there. Updates to these practices will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
